DATA SOURCES

- NASA DONKI (space weather): https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI/api/
- NASA OSDR (space biology): https://osdr.nasa.gov/osdr/data/search
- Mars Trek / MOLA terrain: https://trek.nasa.gov/tiles/Mars/EQ/

DONKI IN THE GAME

- Events from the last two years are loaded.
- Each mission day randomly selects a date with events and displays one real event from that date.
- Dates are not repeated during a mission until all available dates have been used.
- The event date and source link appear with the event.
- Victory and game-over screens list the dates and NASA DONKI source links for events used during the mission.

STARTUP

- Double-click astromission.html to open the start screen in your browser.
- Press any key on astromission.html to open the game page automatically.
- Only astromission.html needs to be opened; the game and its other files load automatically.
